1. Who we are

ManeBooth is an iPhone app provided by Nghia Le, trading as LilGroup ("LilGroup", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, where it is kept and the choices you have. It applies to the ManeBooth iOS app and this website.

For users in the EU, EEA and UK, LilGroup is the "controller" of the limited personal data we process. You can reach us at oscar.lehuu@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your iPhone

ManeBooth works without an account. What you create in the app stays on your device:

  • your selfie, and the answers you give about your hair (length, texture, goal);
  • the haircut photos you paste, pick or take;
  • your looks, photobooth strips, side and back views, stylist cards and cut notes;
  • salon visit dates you choose, and your app settings;
  • a random install ID, kept in your iPhone's Keychain, so our server can count your looks without knowing who you are.

We cannot access this data on your phone.

3. Making a look: what is sent and why

ManeBooth's main feature needs to process photos on a server. Each time you try a haircut, re-try a look, open the photobooth strip or make a stylist card, the app sends over an encrypted connection (HTTPS):

  • your selfie and the haircut photo, shrunk to at most 1024 pixels on the long side;
  • the options you chose (for example keep my colour or keep my length) and your hair answers;
  • your random install ID, and, for paid features, Apple's signed record of your purchase so we can check your plan.

Our server runs on Cloudflare. It forwards the photos to fal.ai, our image provider, which runs the image model (OpenAI's GPT Image, or ByteDance's Seedream if the first one fails) and returns the result as a private link. For stylist cut notes, the result image and the haircut photo are sent to OpenRouter, which runs a text model (OpenAI) that writes the notes. The app then downloads the result to your phone.

Our server does not save your photos. It only works with them in memory for the few seconds a request takes. To avoid charging you twice for the same request, it stores a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the inputs, which cannot be turned back into a photo.

ManeBooth does not try to recognise who you are. We do not create face templates or other biometric identifiers, and we do not compare your face with any other face.

Haircut photos you add may show another person. They are used only to copy the hairstyle onto your own photo; please add only photos you have the right to use (see our Terms of Use).

4. What our server keeps

  • Your random install ID, your plan, how many looks you have used this week and any look-pack credit, so limits work and failed tries are never counted.
  • Look records: a look ID, the private link to the result image, which model made it, and retry counts. Stylist notes text and links to side and back views are kept with the look.
  • Purchase records: Apple transaction IDs and product IDs, so one purchase cannot be used twice.
  • Request fingerprints (hashes) for the duplicate check described above.
  • A per-minute request counter keyed by your install ID and your IP address, to stop abuse.
  • Basic request logs kept by Cloudflare (time, route, status and error messages, not your photos).

None of this includes your name, email, Apple ID or your photos. How long each item is kept is in How long we keep data.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions and the look pack are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or your Apple ID. We use RevenueCat to manage purchases: it receives your random install ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device information (such as iOS version and country) needed to validate purchases.

6. Camera, Photos and notifications

  • Camera: only if you choose to take a selfie or a photo of a haircut. You can pick from Photos instead.
  • Photos: ManeBooth uses Apple's photo picker, so it only sees the photos you choose. Saving a look or a stylist card to Photos asks for permission to add photos only.
  • Notifications: salon reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They do not go through our server.

7. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. The app's help email fills in your app version and iOS version, and the look ID when there is one, to help us find the problem; you can remove them before sending. We use this only to answer you.

8. What we never do

  • We never sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We never use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across other apps and websites.
  • We never use your photos or looks to train models, and never use them for anything other than making your looks.
  • We never store your photos on our server.
  • We never publish or share your looks. Sharing only happens when you tap Share or Send to my salon.

10. How long we keep data

  • Your photos: not kept on our server at all. Our providers may keep request data for a limited time under their own policies.
  • Look records, links to results, stylist notes and view links: deleted from our server 30 days after they are made. Your looks stay on your phone until you delete them.
  • Request fingerprints: 7 days.
  • Per-minute request counters (install ID and IP address): deleted within 24 hours.
  • Cloudflare request logs: a few days.
  • Install ID, look counts and purchase records: while you use ManeBooth, until you delete your data in the app or ask us to.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to help you, then deleted within 12 months.

11. Deleting your data and your choices

  • Delete your selfie: Settings, then Your photo, then Delete.
  • Delete everything: Settings, then Delete all my photos and data. This removes your photos, looks, cards and settings from your iPhone and asks our server to delete the records it holds for your install ID.
  • Delete the app: removes everything stored on your phone. Server records then expire as described above, or you can email us to delete them sooner.
  • Turn off permissions for Camera, Photos and Notifications at any time in iPhone Settings.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

12. Age limits and children

ManeBooth is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please use ManeBooth only with permission from a parent or guardian.

ManeBooth is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help delete any data we hold.

13. Security

Data on your iPhone is protected by iOS security, including your passcode and device encryption. Photos travel over encrypted connections and are not stored on our server. Results are hosted at long, unguessable private links. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum to reduce risk.

14. International transfers

Our providers, including Cloudflare, fal.ai, OpenRouter and RevenueCat, are based in or process data in the United States, so your photos and purchase information may be processed there. When personal data from the EU, EEA or UK is transferred to the US, we rely on the safeguards our providers offer under GDPR Chapter V, in particular the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum or International Data Transfer Agreement for UK data) or, where a provider is certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
